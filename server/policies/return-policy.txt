NovaMart Return and Replacement Policy (fictional sample)

1 Eligibility
1.1 Customers may request a replacement within 14 days of delivery for products that arrive damaged.
1.2 A replacement request must include a photo showing the damage and the order ID.

2 Verification
2.1 Replacement requests are subject to verification by a support agent before approval.
2.2 Damage caused after delivery is not covered by the replacement policy.

3 Wrong or missing items
3.1 If the wrong item is delivered, NovaMart will arrange a pickup and send the correct item at no cost.
3.2 Missing items must be reported within 7 days of delivery.

4 Refunds
4.1 Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after an approved return.
4.2 Refunds for items not received require tracking confirmation from the carrier.
